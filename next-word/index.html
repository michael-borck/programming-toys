The lesson: nothing in there wants to say anything — at 0 it repeats its favourite word forever, above 0 it rolls dice you just watched. Real models do exactly this with about 50,000 word-pieces, a trillion-word reading habit, and thousands of rolls per answer — which is why the same request gives your classmate a different app, and why sounding fluent was never evidence of thinking.
