# Next Word

**Kills:** "it thinks like me."

The whole job of the model inside every AI agent, made visible: given these words, pick the next
one. Watch the candidate shortlist with live probability bars, roll the dice one word at a time,
and drag the dial every AI product ships under the name *temperature* — from **0 · Boring**
(the same sentence, word for word, forever — the history list proves it) to **3 · Unhinged**
(a business must apologise to the ocean). Nothing in there decided anything: it was the
favourite word, or it was dice. It isn't answering you — it's continuing you.

**Pairs with:** Lab 1 — *Hello, Department of One* (`programming-labs`), where the reveal prompt
*"what did you decide that I never asked for?"* gets its mechanical answer.

projector.
