# Demo Illusion

**Kills:** "the demo is the product."

A shiny one-shot bill splitter that genuinely works — you watch the demo land, "$30.00 each",
ship it Friday. Then you let real users in, one at a time, and the same unchanged app prints
`$NaN each`, invoices `$-21.13`, divides by zero, and loses its button off the edge of Rosa's
phone. Nothing is staged: the strangers type into the real app and the page runs its real
two-line brain (shown at the bottom, so you can check). The finale converts every failure into
one plain done-means sentence — a spec being born from the wreckage.

**Pairs with:** Lab 2 — *The Demo Illusion* (`programming-labs`) — this toy is the lab's handoff
disaster in ninety guaranteed seconds; the lab then does it for real with the student's own
product and a borrowed human.
