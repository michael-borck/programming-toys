# Confidence Dial

**Kills:** "fluent = true."

An experiment run on you: eight claims from your agent's end-of-day report, one voice, four
true. Grade each one Trust or Doubt, reveal, and meet your score — most people land within a
whisker of a coin flip, because the cues everyone uses point the wrong way on purpose: the most
confident and most specific claims ("all 1,024 combinations", "34% faster") are false, and the
hedged, weak-sounding one is true. Every reveal names the small, boring check that would have
settled it in seconds. Even a perfect score gets no medal: count what eight judgements cost you
— a checklist does it in seconds without being clever. Stop grading prose; start running checks.

**Pairs with:** Lab 7 — *Trust, Then Verify* (`programming-labs`), where a green tick that
tests nothing is waiting.
