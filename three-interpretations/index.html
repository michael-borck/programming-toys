Now say what you meant — add the words you thought were obvious

Each line is plain language. Watch what your words do to the agent’s freedom to guess.

The lesson: a vague brief doesn’t produce a wrong product — it produces a space of right ones, and the agent picks from it without telling you it picked. Writing down what you meant, precisely enough that a stranger builds the right thing, is programming’s newest notation. It’s called a spec, and you just wrote one with six clicks.
